Good to know

Six things that save your clothes.

Short, plain, and worth remembering the next time you stand in front of the machine.

Fold it warm, straight off the line.

Clothes folded while still slightly warm from the sun barely need ironing. Shake each piece once before you peg it — it dries flatter and smells better.
