Care-symbol decoder

That little label, in plain words.

Every symbol on a care label, drawn the way it looks on the tag — with what it actually means and what to do. Tap any card for the full note.

Stain SOS

Spilled something? Don't panic. Don't rub.

Pick the stain, follow the steps in order. The golden rule for almost all of them: cold water first, hot water later — heat sets a stain for good.

Cost checker

What does one load actually cost?

Move the sliders to match your home. Prices are honest estimates — detergent, power and water at Bengaluru rates — so you can compare washing at home with the laundry shop down the road.
